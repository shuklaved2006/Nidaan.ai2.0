Simulation Pack 2 - Digital Twin of Karnataka Crime Ecosystem
Purpose: Define the living synthetic world that will generate all FIRs, investigations, relationships and intelligence outputs. This document defines recurring entities only. AI outputs are NOT pre-generated.
Simulation Philosophy
The simulation models a realistic policing ecosystem between 1 Jan 2022 and 26 Jul 2026.
Every major entity should recur across multiple events. Crime narratives generate FIRs; FIRs are not created independently.
Criminal Ecosystem
180 criminals
• Tier A: 8 High Value Targets (organised crime, graph hubs)
• Tier B: 25 Repeat Offenders
• Tier C: 147 Opportunistic Offenders

Each criminal has:
Alias, Home District, Modus Operandi, Active Years, Known Associates, Arrest History, Bail History and Speciality.
Victims
320 victims covering residential, commercial, elderly, women, students and businesses.
15–20 recurring victims are intentionally reused across related narratives.
Complainants
250 complainants including neighbours, relatives, security guards, employers, shop managers and witnesses.

80 officers with:

Investigation Speciality

Approx. 80 businesses including jewellery shops, banks, ATMs, warehouses, malls, restaurants, medical stores, petrol pumps and construction sites.
Public Infrastructure
Metro stations, railway stations, bus terminals, hospitals, schools, colleges, markets, parks and government buildings.

Relationship Rules
Every important entity must connect with at least three others.

Example:
Criminal → Gang → Vehicle → Phone → FIR → Victim → Officer → Court → District

No isolated records should exist.
Persons of Interest (POIs)
Introduce POIs who are NOT accused but repeatedly appear through analytics.
Examples:
Repeated CCTV appearances
Multiple social media mentions
Association with known offenders
Vehicle near crime scenes
Frequent contact with suspects
The system may elevate or eliminate them during investigation.
Implementation Rule
This pack defines recurring entities only.
FIRs, arrests, charge sheets and social media events will be generated later from these entities.
