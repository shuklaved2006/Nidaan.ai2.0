Police Stations
Approx. 35 stations distributed across the 12 districts. Bengaluru: Ashok Nagar, Cubbon Park, Indiranagar, Whitefield, Electronic City, Jayanagar, Yelahanka, Hebbal. Mysuru: Nazarbad, Devaraja, Vijayanagar. Mangaluru: North, South, Kadri. Hubballi-Dharwad: Hubballi Town, Vidyanagar, Dharwad Sub Urban. Belagavi: Camp, Market, Tilakwadi. Remaining districts: ~2 each.
Courts
15 courts including District Courts plus Cyber Crime Special Court, Women Fast Track Court and Juvenile Court.
Officer Hierarchy
DGP → ADGP → IGP → DIG → SP → ASP → ACP → Inspector → Sub Inspector → ASI → Head Constable → Constable
Officer Distribution
~80 officers. Bengaluru 30, Mysuru 10, Mangaluru 8, Hubballi-Dharwad 8, Belagavi 6, Remaining districts 18.

Geographic Rules
All coordinates must lie within Karnataka. Natural clustering around Bengaluru, Mysuru, Mangaluru and Hubballi.
ID Convention
DIST001, PS001, OFF0001, CR0001, VIC0001, COM0001, FIR2022-000001, CRT001
Implementation Rules
1. Do not preload AI outputs (hotspots, alerts, patrol recommendations, risk scores).
2. Only simulate raw operational data.
3. Every FIR must belong to one interconnected crime narrative.
4. Reuse people and locations across the five-year timeline.
5. Maintain referential integrity for all IDs.
