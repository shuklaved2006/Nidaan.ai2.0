Simulation Pack 3 - Crime Narratives & Timeline
Purpose: Define the interconnected crime stories that generate FIRs between 1 Jan 2022 and 26 Jul 2026. Every FIR, arrest, chargesheet and social signal will belong to one of these narratives.
Primary Narrative 1 - Operation Night Glass
Residential burglary gang operating across Bengaluru and Mysuru.
Duration: 2022-2026
Features: Repeat offenders, common modus operandi (glass cutting), jewellery theft, recurring victims, hotspot emergence.
Primary Narrative 2 - Chain Riders
Motorcycle chain-snatching group.
Duration: 2023-2026
Features: Shared motorcycle network, repeat offenders, public social-media complaints, movement across police stations.
Primary Narrative 3 - Cipher Network
Cyber fraud ring using phishing and UPI scams.
Duration: 2024-2026
Features: Multiple complainants, shared bank accounts, common phone numbers, cross-district victims.
Primary Narrative 4 - Falcon Auto Theft
Organised vehicle theft targeting two-wheelers.
Features: Chop-shop links, repeated disposal patterns, interstate movement simulation.
Primary Narrative 5 - Silent Streets
Street-light failures followed by theft increase.
Features: Citizen complaints, local news, environmental correlation.
Primary Narrative 6 - Festival Shield
Festival crowd management and opportunistic theft.
Features: Public safety monitoring, crowd intelligence, theft clusters during major events.
Primary Narrative 7 - Shadow Extortion
Business extortion affecting shops and warehouses.
Features: Business victims, repeat intimidation, organised network.
Primary Narrative 8 - Missing Threads
Missing persons with links to trafficking investigation.
Features: Multi-agency coordination, timeline reconstruction.
Secondary Narratives
12 supporting stories covering:
• Domestic violence
• Illegal racing
• Assault
• Drug possession
• Public vandalism
• ATM theft
• Construction site theft
• School-area incidents
• Commercial burglary
• Fraud against elderly
• Market pickpocketing
• Public disorder
Timeline Rules
Every narrative progresses through:
Incident → FIR → Investigation → Arrest (where applicable) → Chargesheet → Court.
Not every case reaches every stage by July 2026; active investigations remain open.
Social Intelligence Mapping
Only selected narratives receive supporting X, Instagram, news or community posts.
Social content must precede or coincide with incidents—not reference future outcomes.
Expected Platform Discoveries
These are validation goals, not stored data.
The application should discover:
• Hotspots
• Repeat offenders
• Criminal associations
• Similar modus operandi
• Emerging trends
• Explainable risk indicators
• Patrol recommendations
